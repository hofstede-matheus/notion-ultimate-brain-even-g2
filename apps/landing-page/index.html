Even Hub is open.

Even Hub is live and ready for your code. You have everything you need to build, test, and publish applications directly to the Even G2. Move from concept to deployment instantly, and put your unobtrusive, high-utility tools right into the user's field of view.
Developer tools

Simple SDK to interact with glasses.

Build for the Even G2 with ease. The Even Hub SDK provides high-level APIs that handle the hardware and system details for you, so you can focus on your app's behavior. With a quick setup and support for local development, you can iterate rapidly to discover what feels right on a heads-up display - from text that's readable at a glance to interactions that stay out of the way.
