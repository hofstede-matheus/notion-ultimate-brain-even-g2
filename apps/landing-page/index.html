Your Notion,
on your face.

Tasks

Run GTD from your glasses.

Work your Inbox, Today, Tomorrow, and Next 7 days lists without touching your phone. Open a task's action menu to reschedule it on a bitmap-drawn month calendar, reassign its project, mark it done, or delete it - all from the heads-up display.
Notes, projects & tags

Browse everything, organized.

Notes by tag, project, meeting, or voice note. Projects by Doing, Ongoing, Planned, On hold, and Done. Tags by Area, Resource, or Entity. Drill into any of them to see what's filed underneath, then act on an item right from its menu.
Voice capture

Capture a task, hands-free.

Dictate a new task offline, on-device, confirm the transcript, and it's written straight to your Notion Inbox. No phone, no app switching, no losing the thought before you can write it down.
Page reader

Read the full page, on-screen.

A task's or note's page renders as pre-paginated screenfuls of text, turned with a swipe - falling back to the Description property when a page's body is empty, as most Ultimate Brain tasks are.
Your data

Your Notion, your rules.

Multi-tenant by design: each device holds its own Notion integration token and database IDs, sent with every request. The backend is a stateless proxy - it never stores your Notion credentials.
Open source

Free, and yours to run.

The glasses app is free; deploy your own backend and read the source on GitHub. It's a Turborepo + pnpm workspaces monorepo, built for the Even Realities G2.
